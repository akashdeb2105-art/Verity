# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a good project:

- Being welcoming to people who are new to the problem, the codebase, or both
- Disagreeing about technical decisions without disparaging the person
- Accepting review as an improvement to the work rather than a judgement of the author
- Saying plainly when you are unsure, and correcting yourself when you were wrong

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Insults, personal attacks, or sustained dismissiveness
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Anything a reasonable colleague would consider inappropriate in a workplace

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, code comments — and whenever an individual is representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/akashdeb2105-art/Verity/security/advisories/new),
which is the most private channel this repository offers. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and
will take fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they consider
inappropriate. They will respect the privacy and security of anyone who reports
an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
